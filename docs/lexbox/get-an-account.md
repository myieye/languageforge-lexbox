One Lexbox account signs you in everywhere: the lexbox.org website, Send/Receive in
FieldWorks or WeSay, and FieldWorks Lite.

## Register

Go to [lexbox.org](https://lexbox.org) and choose **Register**. You need a name, an email
address and a password — or use **Register with Google** to skip the password. Your email
address is also your Send/Receive login.

Lexbox then sends you a verification email. Follow its link soon: until your address is
verified you cannot join or request a project. Use **Resend verification email** on the
banner if it never arrived.

If you used Language Depot in the past, your account still works — log in with your email
address or your old Send/Receive username.

## Join your team's project

You don't attach yourself to a project; a **project manager** adds you. Tell your manager
the email address you registered with, and the project appears under **My Projects** the
next time you log in. Managers can also invite people who have no account yet — the
invitation email contains a **Join Project** button that walks you through registering.

## Your account settings

**Account Settings** (under your name, top right) lets you change your display name, your
email address (the new address must be verified before it takes over) and the website's
display language.

To change your password, use **Reset your password instead?** on that page — or **Forgot
your password?** on the login page if you are locked out.
