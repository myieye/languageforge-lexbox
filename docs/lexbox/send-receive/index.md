**Send/Receive** is how desktop programs exchange work with Lexbox: it uploads your
changes and downloads your colleagues', merging the two. You run it from inside your
program — FieldWorks, WeSay, OneStory Editor, OurWord or Adapt It — not from the
website.

You need three things:

- your Lexbox **login** (your email address, or an old Send/Receive username) and
  **password**;
- to be a **member** of the project, as an Editor or Manager (see
  [Members and roles](/lexbox/manage-members));
- the **project code**, shown on the project's page on lexbox.org (for example
  `abc-flex`).

The easiest path: open your project's page on lexbox.org and choose **Get project**. It
shows the steps for your program with your login and project code already filled in.

## FieldWorks

To get a project onto your computer for the first time:

1. In FieldWorks, choose **Send/Receive** → **Get Project from colleague…**.
2. Choose **Internet**.
3. Enter your login and password.
4. Pick your project from the list (by its project code).
5. Choose where to put it on your computer.

After that, **Send/Receive** in FieldWorks syncs the project whenever you choose. For
how Send/Receive itself works — merging, conflict notes, good habits — see the
[FieldWorks help](https://software.sil.org/fieldworks/).

## WeSay

In the **WeSay Configuration Tool**, choose **Get from Internet** and enter the project
code, your login and your password. One known quirk: WeSay mishandles the `@` sign in
logins, so type `%40` in its place — `name%40example.com`.

## Slow or unreliable internet?

Send/Receive is built for bad connections and can pick up where it left off, so a large
project can arrive over several attempts. If your team is stuck, write to
[admin@languagedepot.org](mailto:admin@languagedepot.org).
