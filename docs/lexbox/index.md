Lexbox is the online home for your team's language projects. It stores each project on a
server, keeps everyone's copies in step, and gives you a website —
[lexbox.org](https://lexbox.org) — for managing who is on a project. Lexbox used to be
called **Language Depot**; it is the same service, and old accounts and projects are
still there.

Lexbox hosts projects for **FieldWorks** (the desktop app, often called FLEx — FieldWorks
Lite calls it *FieldWorks Classic*), **WeSay**, **OneStory Editor**, **OurWord** and
**Adapt It**. Those programs exchange work with Lexbox through **Send/Receive**. Lexbox is
free to use, and is provided by SIL Global and the Linguistics Institute at Payap
University.

:::tip[Do you use FieldWorks Lite?]
Then you rarely need this guide — syncing happens inside the app, and the
[FieldWorks Lite user guide](/fw-lite/) covers everyday work. This guide is for the
website and for Send/Receive programs.
:::

## In this guide

- [Get an account](/lexbox/get-an-account) — register, verify your email and join your
  team's project.
- [Host a project](/lexbox/host-a-project) — get a new project set up on Lexbox.
- [Send/Receive](/lexbox/send-receive/) — connect FieldWorks or WeSay to your project.
- [Members and roles](/lexbox/manage-members) — add people to a project and choose what
  they can do.
- [Organizations](/lexbox/organizations) — group your projects and people.
- [Use FieldWorks Lite with your project](/lexbox/fw-lite) — for project managers whose
  team wants the lightweight app too.

Need help? The **Help** menu on lexbox.org has step-by-step guides, or you can write to
[admin@languagedepot.org](mailto:admin@languagedepot.org).
