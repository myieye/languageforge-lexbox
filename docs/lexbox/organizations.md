An organization groups the projects and people that belong together — a language
program, an institution, a partner organization. Find yours under **Organizations** in
the top bar.

Organizations are created by the Lexbox team; write to
[admin@languagedepot.org](mailto:admin@languagedepot.org) if yours needs one.

## Roles

An organization has **Admins** and **Members**. Admins can rename the organization,
add and remove people, and manage every project the organization owns — they count as
managers on all of them, including confidential ones. Members can see the
organization's projects and ask to join them.

## Managing

From the organization's page:

- **Add Member** adds a person by email or login; if they don't have an account yet,
  tick **Invite** to send them an invitation email instead. (Newly added members may
  need to log out and back in before they see the organization.)
- **Bulk Add Members** takes a list of logins or emails — unlike the project version,
  it does not create accounts for people who don't have one.
- Any member can use **Add My Projects** to bring projects they manage into the
  organization, and can **Leave organization** — except the last admin.

When you [create a project](/lexbox/host-a-project), you can assign it to your
organization on the form.
