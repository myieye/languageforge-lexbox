An organization groups the projects and people that belong together: a language program,
an institution, a partner organization. Find yours under **Organizations** in the top bar.

Organizations are created by the Lexbox team. Write to
[admin@languagedepot.org](mailto:admin@languagedepot.org) if yours needs one.

## Roles

An organization has **Admins** and **Members**. Admins can rename the organization, add
and remove people, and manage every project the organization owns. They count as managers
on all of them, including confidential ones. Members can see the organization's projects
that are marked not confidential (plus their own), and ask to join them.

## Managing

From the organization's page:

- **Add Member** adds a person by email or login. If they don't have an account yet, tick
  **Invite** to send them an invitation email instead. Admins can also create an account
  for someone with **Create User**.
- **Bulk Add Members** takes a list of logins or emails. Unlike the project version, it
  does not create accounts for people who don't have one.
- Any member can use **Add My Projects** to bring projects they manage into the
  organization, and can **Leave organization** (on the **Settings** tab), except the last
  admin.

When you [create a project](/lexbox/host-a-project), you can assign it to your
organization on the form.
