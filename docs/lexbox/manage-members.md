Every project has a member list, managed on the project's page on lexbox.org. Members
sign in with their own accounts — teams should not share one login.

## Roles

| Role | What it allows |
| --- | --- |
| **Manager** | Send/Receive, plus managing the project: members, name, description, settings. |
| **Editor** | Send/Receive. |
| **Viewer** | Looking at the project in FieldWorks Lite, without editing. Offered only on FieldWorks projects. |

## Add people

As a manager, use **Add/Invite Member** on the project page: enter the person's email
address (or old Send/Receive login) and pick a role. If they don't have an account yet,
tick **Invite** — they get an invitation email with a **Join Project** button, which
expires after a few days.

For a whole class or workshop, **Bulk Add/Create Members** takes a list of logins or
emails, one per line, and a shared password; accounts are created automatically for
people who don't have one.

Members of an organization that owns your project can also find it themselves and
**Ask to join** — you'll get an email with an **Approve Request** button.

Each member's menu lets you **Change Role** or **Remove** them. Anyone can leave a
project themselves (**Leave Project**, under More settings) — except the last manager,
who must first hand the project to someone else.

## Confidential projects

A manager can mark a project as confidential (**Set project confidentiality**). On a
confidential project, only managers see the member list, and the project is hidden from
people who aren't members.

## Project history

The project page shows the recent changes sent to Lexbox — when, by whom, and the
message their program attached. **Open in HgWeb** shows the complete history.
