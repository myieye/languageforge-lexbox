**FieldWorks Lite** is a companion app for the dictionary part of a FieldWorks project:
fast, simple, and it runs on phones, tablets and computers. Team members who only do
dictionary work, and would never open FieldWorks itself, can work in FieldWorks Lite while
the rest of the team keeps using FieldWorks and Send/Receive. (FieldWorks Lite calls the
desktop program *FieldWorks Classic*.)

This page is for the project manager who sets that up. The app itself is covered in the
[FieldWorks Lite user guide](/fw-lite/).

## Turn it on for your project

FieldWorks Lite works with FieldWorks projects that already have data on Lexbox. A new
project stays empty until someone sends it from FieldWorks for the first time
([Fill it with data](/lexbox/host-a-project#fill-it-with-data)), so even a team that will
only use FieldWorks Lite needs FieldWorks once to start.

On your project's page on lexbox.org, choose **Try FieldWorks Lite?** and confirm with
**Yes, please!**. Lexbox prepares a FieldWorks Lite copy of your project; the first time
can take a few minutes on a large project. Afterwards the project carries a **Using
FieldWorks Lite** badge, and your team can
[install the app](https://lexbox.org/fw-lite) and
[download the project](/fw-lite/getting-started#download-your-project).

FieldWorks Lite support is still in beta, so only people with early access see the
button, and only a project manager can confirm it. Managers can request early access on
the [Where's my project?](https://lexbox.org/wheresMyProject) page.

## Keep the two sides in step

Your project now lives in Lexbox in two copies: one for FieldWorks Lite, one for
FieldWorks Send/Receive. The **Sync** button in the app (under **Synchronize**, on the
**Lexbox** tab) merges the two, and any Editor or Manager can press it. People with early
access also see **Sync FieldWorks Lite** on the project page, which does exactly the same
thing. Neither runs on its own, so make it part of the team's rhythm:

1. FieldWorks users **Send/Receive** as usual.
2. Someone presses **Sync** in the app (or **Sync FieldWorks Lite** on the project page).
3. FieldWorks users **Send/Receive** again to pick up the FieldWorks Lite work.

FieldWorks Lite users don't do anything: the app syncs with Lexbox on its own. To see the
whole journey of an edit, step through [How sync works](/fw-lite/how-sync-works).
