**FieldWorks Lite** is a companion app for the dictionary part of a FieldWorks project:
fast, simple, and it runs on phones, tablets and computers. Team members who only do
dictionary work — and would never open FieldWorks itself — can work in FieldWorks Lite
while the rest of the team keeps using FieldWorks and Send/Receive.

This page is for the project manager who sets that up. The app itself is covered in the
[FieldWorks Lite user guide](/fw-lite/).

## Turn it on for your project

On your project's page on lexbox.org, choose **Try FieldWorks Lite?**. Lexbox prepares a
FieldWorks Lite copy of your project; the first time can take a few minutes on a large
project. Afterwards the project carries a **Using FieldWorks Lite** badge, and your team
can [install the app and download the project](/fw-lite/getting-started).

FieldWorks Lite support is still in beta. If you don't see the button, a project manager
can request early access from the project page.

## Keep the two sides in step

Your project now lives in Lexbox in two copies: one for FieldWorks Lite, one for
FieldWorks Send/Receive. The **Sync FieldWorks Lite** button on the project page merges
the two — and it only runs when someone presses it (in the website or in the app), so
make it part of the team's rhythm:

1. FieldWorks users **Send/Receive** as usual.
2. Someone presses **Sync FieldWorks Lite**.
3. FieldWorks users **Send/Receive** again to pick up the FieldWorks Lite work.

FieldWorks Lite users don't do anything — the app syncs with Lexbox on its own. To see
the whole journey of an edit, step through [How sync works](/fw-lite/how-sync-works).
